Marmalade Library

Browser-independent catalog retention for marmaladeum.com. The extension backs up library names, videos, folders and metadata automatically. Chrome/Edge can additionally remember authorized folder handles. Firefox and Safari may require selecting files again after restart. Your video bytes are never uploaded or copied into extension storage.
